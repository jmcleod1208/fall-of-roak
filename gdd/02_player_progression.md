# 02 Player Progression

## Purpose

Define how the player, party, Homefront, and campaign grow over time.

## Status

**Design State:** Locked initial player progression.

**Last Locked Update:** 2026-05-31

## Locked Inputs

- **Project:** Fall of Ro'ak.
- **Genre Frame:** Auto-battler and town-management idle RPG.
- **Combat Input:** Setup-only auto-battler combat.
- **Homefront:** Gameplay/UI term for the broader rebuilding network that supports the party.
- **Hero Structure:** Four fixed main heroes, with recruited followers supporting them.
- **Lore Source:** Lore bible remains the source of truth for canon.

## Progression Summary

Progression in **Fall of Ro'ak** is driven by gear, Homefront building growth, story advancement, and logistical strength rather than traditional character leveling.

The four main heroes stay fixed and progress primarily through gear. Recruited followers also progress through gear only, keeping them useful but replaceable. The Homefront progresses through individual building upgrades, multiple rebuilding projects, and resource production chains. New campaign content unlocks mainly through story gates.

## Hero Progression

The four main heroes do not rely on traditional level growth as their primary progression path. Their power and role development come mainly from gear, equipment choices, and loadouts.

Hero progression includes:

- Weapons.
- Armor.
- Accessories.
- Role-defining equipment.
- Combat loadouts.
- Potential relics or artifacts.
- Synergy with followers and formations.

This keeps the four heroes stable as lore characters while still allowing their battlefield function to evolve mechanically.

## Follower Progression

Recruited followers progress through gear only.

Followers do not use deep individual level systems because they can be injured and killed. Their value comes from:

- Their role.
- Their equipment.
- Their formation position.
- Their synergy with the four heroes.
- Their contribution to repeat farming, campaign battles, and resource gathering.

Because followers can die, their gear has a special loss rule. When a follower dies, their gear is sent back to the Homefront. The gear is badly damaged and cannot be equipped again. The blacksmith can break the damaged gear down into materials.

This makes follower death meaningful without permanently deleting all value from the player's investment.

## Gear Progression

Gear can be crafted and looted.

Gear cannot be upgraded in any way. Its value comes from acquiring, crafting, looting, choosing, equipping, replacing, and salvaging it rather than enhancing a single item forever.

Locked gear rules:

- Gear can be crafted.
- Gear can be looted.
- Gear cannot be upgraded.
- Gear cannot be enhanced.
- Gear cannot be socketed.
- Gear cannot be improved after creation or acquisition.
- Damaged gear from dead followers cannot be reused.
- Damaged follower gear can be broken down by the blacksmith into materials.

This keeps gear progression focused on finding and making better equipment rather than investing endlessly into one piece.

## Homefront Progression

The Homefront does not have one overall level.

Homefront progression comes from:

- Individual building levels.
- Multiple rebuilding projects.
- Resource production chains.

Building upgrades unlock or improve production, logistics, recovery, recruitment, crafting, supplies, caravan support, blacksmith salvage, and storage.

Specific buildings and building upgrades can also grant passive stat bonuses to the party. These bonuses come from individual buildings or their upgrades, not from any overall Homefront level. A rebuilt or upgraded building can improve a relevant party stat for as long as it stands, tying combat strength back to which parts of the rebuilding network the player invests in.

The lack of a single Homefront level keeps progression more concrete. The player improves specific parts of the rebuilding network instead of raising one abstract number.

## Campaign Progression

Campaign progression is mainly gated by story.

New content unlocks through:

- Story milestones.
- Region introductions.
- Major campaign objectives.
- Defeating key enemies.
- Reaching important lore locations.
- Advancing the march east.
- Progressing toward Ro'ak and Noctheris.

Power and resources can still support campaign advancement, but they should not be the main unlock method. The story determines when new regions, enemies, systems, and major features become available.

## Difficulty Progression

Difficulty should increase as the heroes travel farther east.

This can include:

- Longer caravan travel times.
- Stronger enemy groups.
- More dangerous enemy abilities.
- Greater healing item demand.
- More pressure on followers.
- More complex gear and formation decisions.
- Larger Homefront support needs.
- Higher demand for crafted and looted gear.

The campaign should feel like the party is moving farther from safety and deeper into danger.

## Unlock Progression

Unlocks should come from story gates first, then be supported by Homefront building progression and gear acquisition.

Examples:

- New regions unlock through story.
- New enemy types unlock through story.
- New follower types unlock through story or Homefront recruitment buildings.
- New caravan upgrades unlock at different stages of the game.
- New production chains unlock through individual Homefront buildings.
- New gear tiers unlock through campaign progress, loot, and crafting access.
- New rebuilding projects unlock as the story reaches new damaged regions.
- Blacksmith salvage becomes important when followers begin dying with equipped gear.

## Lore Cross-Reference

This progression model supports the lore bible because the four heroes remain narratively stable. They are not treated as disposable or replaceable, and their growth does not require changing who they are. Gear-driven progression lets their combat roles evolve without contradicting their locked character identities.

Follower gear-only progression fits the darker campaign tone because followers can be lost. The salvage rule reinforces that follower death has weight: their equipment returns damaged and unusable, but the Homefront can reclaim materials through the blacksmith.

Homefront building progression supports the rebuilding theme. The player is not only marching toward Ro'ak, but restoring what his destruction left behind through specific buildings, projects, and production chains.

Story-gated campaign progression keeps the march east, Ro'ak's forces, Noctheris, and the major lore locations in control of the game's structure.
